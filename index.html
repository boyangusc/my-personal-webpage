Bo Yang

I am an Assistant Professor of Management and Strategy at the University of Hong Kong Business School. I am generally interested in how firms manage dynamics in their institutional environments, including regulatory uncertainty, stakeholder activism, and geopolitical tensions, to achieve competitive advantages. Across research projects, I primarily study firms' political strategy, global strategy, and innovation strategy.

Academic keywords: corporate political strategy, corporate sociopolitical activism, shareholder activism, global supply chains, geopolitical risk, political institutions and innovation.
