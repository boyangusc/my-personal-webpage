Bo Yang

I am an Assistant Professor of Management and Strategy at the University of Hong Kong Business School.

I study how firms integrate market and nonmarket strategies to create competitive advantage. More broadly, my research examines how firms navigate and shape their institutional environments to position themselves in evolving technological and (geo)political landscapes. I am also interested in the dynamics of power and legitimacy within these processes.

Research example: One of my dissertation chapters examines how firms use information-seeking lobbying to gather advance information about forthcoming tariff policies and use this informational advantage to strategically stockpile soon-to-be-tariffed imports (Strategic Management Journal).

Current projects: I am developing multiple projects on the interrelationships between firms’ political activities and their supply chain and innovation strategies.

Academic keywords: corporate political strategy, sociopolitical activism, geopolitical risk, global supply chains, institutions and innovation.
